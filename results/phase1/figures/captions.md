## 1.1_baselines_val

**Unpaired baselines fit on the training halves reach centered R² at or below 0, whereas paired methods fit on all training pairs reach up to 0.27.** Each bar is one baseline scored on the 135 validation pairs (14 donors) in single-cell → bulk (orange) and bulk → single-cell (blue). Open bars are unpaired baselines fit on the training halves (halving 0: single-cell profiles from 33 training donors, bulk profiles from 35 others), the comparators for models trained without pairs. Filled bars are paired methods fit on all 582 training pairs (68 donors), shown as a reference for what pairs can buy; the paired multilayer perceptron (MLP) is the mean of 3 seeds. Both groups are scored on the same validation pairs by the same evaluators. (a) Centered R², the median over samples of 1 − SSE/SST with SST taken around the target's per-gene training mean (0 = no better than predicting the training mean; higher is better). (b) Classifier two-sample test (C2ST) AUROC for telling translated from real target profiles; the dashed line marks 0.5, indistinguishable from real. (c) Composition R² of a ridge probe, trained on real training targets, applied to the translations (higher is better); dotted lines give the probe's score on the real validation targets (0.481 bulk, 0.858 single-cell). (d) Within-donor top-1 rate, the fraction of draws whose real partner is the most similar profile among the same donor's draws; the dotted line is chance (0.104). In (a) and (c), hatched bars are cut at −0.5 and their true value is printed on the bar. No unpaired baseline has positive centered R²; the paired methods do, except ridge + PC calibration (ridge with its spread calibrated along each principal component) in single-cell → bulk (−0.051) and the paired MLP in bulk → single-cell (−0.587).

## 1.2_per_sample_accuracy

**Only the paired regressors place most validation samples above the accuracy of the training mean.** Distribution over the 135 validation pairs (14 donors) of each sample's centered R² with its real partner, 1 − ‖ŷ − y‖² / ‖y − μ‖², where μ is the target's per-gene training mean; the dashed line at 0 is the accuracy of predicting μ. (a) Single-cell → bulk; (b) bulk → single-cell. Open boxes are unpaired baselines fit on the training halves (halving 0), the comparators for models trained without pairs; filled boxes are paired methods fit on all training pairs, a reference (paired multilayer perceptron, MLP: seed 0). Boxes span the interquartile range with the median marked; whiskers reach the furthest sample within 1.5 times the interquartile range; outliers are not drawn. The axis is cut at −1.5. Identity and Harmony lie entirely below the axis (medians −14.026 and −14.022 in single-cell → bulk, −43.018 and −42.222 in bulk → single-cell), and mean shift in bulk → single-cell (median −1.750) lies partly below it. Every unpaired baseline has its median at or below 0, while ridge and ridge + spread have medians above 0 in both directions.

## 1.3_translation_pca

**Ridge regression collapses translations toward the center of the real profiles; stretching it, or recoloring the covariance with CORAL, restores spread.** Translated (black) and real (color) validation profiles, 135 pairs from 14 donors, on the first two principal components (PCs) of the target domain, fit on the real training targets. (a) Single-cell → bulk, with real whole-blood bulk in orange; (b) bulk → single-cell, with real single-cell pseudobulk in blue. Columns, left to right: mean shift and CORAL, unpaired baselines fit on the training halves (halving 0); ridge and ridge + spread, paired methods fit on all training pairs (a reference). In single-cell → bulk, mean shift and ridge concentrate their translations near the center of the real cloud (diversity, the mean pairwise distance between translations relative to that between real targets, 0.612 and 0.408), ridge + spread widens them (0.653), and CORAL overshoots (1.125). In bulk → single-cell, ridge collapses (0.470) while mean shift does not (1.470).

## 1.4_accuracy_realism

**No baseline is both accurate and realistic: the unpaired baselines are less accurate than the training mean, and stretching a paired regressor trades accuracy for realism.** Each marker is one baseline scored on the 135 validation pairs (14 donors): centered R² (higher is better; the dashed line at 0 is the accuracy of predicting the target's training mean) against realism, 1 − 2|AUROC − 0.5| of a classifier two-sample test (C2ST) separating translated from real profiles (1 = indistinguishable from real). (a) Single-cell → bulk; (b) bulk → single-cell. Open squares are unpaired baselines fit on the training halves (halving 0), the comparators for models trained without pairs; the faint squares joined to them are the same baselines fit on halving 1, a sensitivity check rather than an independent replicate (the two halvings share 19 of halving 0's 33 single-cell donors). Filled circles are paired methods fit on all training pairs, a reference (paired multilayer perceptron, MLP: mean of 3 seeds). Both groups are scored on the same validation pairs by the same evaluators. The gray curve is ridge with its outputs stretched about the training-target mean by factors 1.0 to 2.5 in steps of 0.1, which changes realism without adding information; ridge + spread is the point on it whose factor (1.6) maximizes the selection score on the training donors. Along the curve, centered R² changes little up to ×1.4 and then falls, while realism peaks at ×1.8 (0.804 in single-cell → bulk, 0.652 in bulk → single-cell). Identity and Harmony (centered R² below −14, C2ST AUROC 1.000) are not shown. For scale, the donor-bootstrap SD of the overall selection score of ridge + spread is 0.035 (200 resamples of the validation donors).
